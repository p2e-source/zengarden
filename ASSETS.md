# Asset provenance

- All garden meshes are original procedural models authored in `src/models.ts`: branching trunks, layered foliage, blossom clusters, sculpted river stones, flowers, pagoda lantern, pond stones, koi, lily pads, ripples, and a plank bridge with curved handrails. Materials and sand grain are generated locally.
- The sand-raking texture is drawn from player input; it is not a fixed decorative image.
- Ambient audio is original Web Audio synthesis in `src/sound.ts`. No sampled recordings or external audio services are used.
- DM Sans is bundled under its SIL Open Font License (`public/fonts/LICENSE.txt`).
- Babylon.js is Apache-2.0; bundled notices are in `public/licenses/`.
- Favicon and interface marks are original code-native artwork.
- `public/art/cover.jpg`, `social.jpg`, and `thumbnail.jpg` are optimized derivatives of original OpenAI ImageGen promotional artwork, generated in built-in mode. They are illustrations, not evidence of gameplay fidelity.

The image brief requested a full miniature Japanese sand garden on a cedar tray, bonsai, cherry blossoms, a jade koi pond, arched bridge, stone lantern, ivory/sage/blush palette, and the exact title “ZEN GARDEN” with “A LITTLE WORLD OF PEACE.” No third-party characters or game assets were used.

Actual gameplay captures are in `screenshots/`.
